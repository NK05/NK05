## The problem space

Schools need operational systems that handle records and financial events consistently, across different users and organisational boundaries. This is more demanding than putting forms and charts on a page: accounting operations need explicit controls, corrections and traceable reporting.

## My work

I have been involved in planning the product and developing its software architecture and application workflows alongside partners. The codebase includes financial-domain logic around balanced journal entries, controlled posting, compensating reversals, period restrictions and organisation-scoped reporting.

Those rules matter because a system should not quietly allow inconsistent entries or let one organisation's records appear in another's reports.

## Engineering focus

- Domain rules enforced on the server, not solely in the interface
- Transactional posting and reversal processes
- Tenant boundaries, permissions and report consistency
- Modular groundwork for operational and user-facing workflows

**Business process understanding → domain modelling → backend controls → usable application workflows.**
